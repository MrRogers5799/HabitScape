WHAT'S COMING

HabitScape is built by one person, in the open. This is the rough plan: ideas, not promises, and no dates, because real life comes first. The order can change based on what you tell me.

NOW: SETTLING INTO 1.0

Fixing whatever you find
  • Bug fixes and polish from your reports If something feels off, tell me on Discord. Small fixes ship fastest.
  • Making your history safe and fast Behind-the-scenes work so streaks and XP stay correct as your history grows.
  • Easier to read and use Clearer text sizes and better screen reader support.

NEXT

The next big update
  • Reminders Optional nudges so a habit doesn't slip through the cracks.
  • A better Profile Your stats, streaks and milestones at a glance.
  • More activities for every skill Including the newer skills, which are a bit thin right now.

LATER

Bigger ideas, still being designed
  • Loot and rare drops A small chance of finding items when you complete habits, from common to very rare.
  • Collection log Somewhere to show off everything you've found.
  • Pets The rarest drops of all.
  • Milestones for skill levels Something special to mark big levels on the way to 99.

Got an idea or want to vote on what comes first? Come say hi on the HabitScape Discord.
