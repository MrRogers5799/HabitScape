WHAT'S COMING

HabitScape is built by one person, in the open. This is the rough plan: not a promise list, and no dates, because real life comes first. Priorities shift based on your feedback.

NOW: SETTLING INTO 1.0

Fixing whatever you find
  • Bug fixes and polish from your reports If something feels off, tell me on Discord. Small fixes ship fastest.
  • Keeping your history safe and fast Behind-the-scenes work so streaks stay correct as your history grows.
  • Easier to read and use Clearer text sizes and better screen reader support.

NEXT: THE NEW HABITSCAPE

The big one, in testing now

Your habits stay at the heart of the app, and now they power an adventurer too. Every habit you tick earns adventure time, and you spend it in a hand-painted valley. A first slice is in testing; it comes out as one big update once every skill and the final art are in. Your habits, streaks and workouts carry over exactly as they are.

  • Your habits earn adventure time Do your routine and your adventurer gets hours to spend.
  • A queue of things to do Line up mining, fishing, smithing, cooking or fights, add more while your adventurer is out, and come back to see what they found. It all happens in real time while you get on with your day.
  • All 24 skills The first slice has 10: Attack, Strength, Defence, Hitpoints, Prayer, Mining, Smithing, Woodcutting, Fishing and Cooking. Still to come: Ranged, Magic, Runecrafting, Crafting, Fletching, Herblore, Agility, Thieving, Firemaking, Slayer, Hunter, Farming, Construction and Sailing.
  • Gear that matters Craft better tools and armour, and watch your rates climb.
  • Quests, village orders and a first boss Quests open up new parts of the map, villagers post requests, and the road to the Bog Warden gives new players a goal from day one.
  • Pets and a collection log A rare pet for every skill, and a log of every rare find.
  • A fresh, illustrated look New original art throughout, with the world itself doing more of the work and fewer menus.
