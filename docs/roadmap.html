WHAT'S COMING

HabitScape is built by one person, in the open. This is the rough plan: not a promise list, and no dates, because real life comes first. Priorities shift based on your feedback.

NOW: SETTLING INTO 1.0

Fixing whatever you find
  • Bug fixes and polish from your reports If something feels off, tell me on Discord. Small fixes ship fastest.
  • Keeping your history safe and fast Behind-the-scenes work so streaks and XP stay correct as your history grows.
  • Easier to read and use Clearer text sizes and better screen reader support.

IN THE PIPELINE

The big stuff I'm working toward
  • Workouts earn XP Finish a workout and its linked skill gets an XP drop.
  • Gear Unlock gear at skill milestones that boosts XP in related skills.
  • Capes at 99 Shown on your profile when you max a skill.
  • Achievement diaries Tiered achievements for real-life goals.
  • Character customisation Build your own adventurer.
  • A new XP curve Early levels fly by; high levels ask you to unlock better ways to train.
  • Reminders Optional nudges so a habit doesn't slip through the cracks.

BEING CONSIDERED

Not planned yet, but on my mind
  • Loot and rare drops A small chance of finding something when you complete a habit, plus a log to show it all off.
  • Quests
  • Social features Compare progress with friends.

Got an idea or want to vote on what comes first? Come say hi on the HabitScape Discord.
