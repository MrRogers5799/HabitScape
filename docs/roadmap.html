WHAT'S COMING

HabitScape is built by one person, in the open. This is the rough plan: not a promise list, and no dates, because real life comes first. Priorities shift based on your feedback.

NOW: SETTLING INTO 1.0

Fixing whatever you find
  • Bug fixes and polish from your reports If something feels off, tell me on Discord. Small fixes ship fastest.
  • Keeping your history safe and fast Behind-the-scenes work so streaks stay correct as your history grows.
  • Easier to read and use Clearer text sizes and better screen reader support.

NEXT: ADVENTURE MODE (BETA)

The big one I'm working toward

Your habits stay at the heart of the app, and now they power an adventurer too. It'll start as an opt-in tab, so nothing you use today changes unless you want it to.

  • Your habits earn adventure time Do your routine and your adventurer gets hours to spend.
  • Send them on trips Mine, fish, chop, smith, cook or fight while you get on with your day, then come back to see what they found.
  • Gear that matters Craft better tools and armour from Bronze up to Steel, and watch your rates climb.
  • A first boss With its own rare drops, and a pet if you're lucky.
  • Quests That open up new parts of the map.
  • Reminders Optional nudges so a habit doesn't slip through the cracks.

LATER

Once adventure mode has found its feet
  • Ranged and Magic, more regions, and gear beyond Steel
  • Slayer tasks, farming at your camp, and building up your camp
  • Skilling outfits, and capes at 99
  • Sailing and the islands
  • A fresh, illustrated look for the whole game

BEING CONSIDERED

  • Workouts in the game For now the workout logger stays its own thing.
  • Achievement diaries Tiered goals to chase.
  • Character customisation Make your adventurer your own.
  • Social features Compare progress with friends.
